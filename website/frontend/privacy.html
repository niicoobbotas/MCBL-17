1. Who we are and how to contact us

SmartEV is a research prototype developed by Team 12 at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e) as part of the 4CBLW00 course. The data controller for personal data collected through this service is Team 12, reachable at privacy@smartev.nl.

For data protection queries, please use the same address. We aim to respond within 72 hours.

2. What data we collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the smart-charging service:

Account data

  • Full name and email address (used for login and account identification)
  • Hashed password (we never store your password in plaintext; bcrypt work-factor 12)
  • Energy supplier preference (Eneco or Essent)
  • Charging profile preference (Budget Saver / Smart Balance / Grid Hero)
  • Typical plug-in time and ready-by deadline
  • Date and time your account was created
  • Your GDPR consent timestamp

Vehicle data

  • EV model name, battery capacity (kWh), WLTP range, charging power, and efficiency — as entered by you from the catalogue or manually

Charging session data

  • Session date and time
  • Energy delivered (kWh), session cost (€), savings vs. fixed contract (€)
  • Plug-in and deadline hours
  • The CACCS-computed schedule (JSON, stored for billing reference)

Technical data

  • IP address — collected temporarily during authentication to enforce login rate limits (not stored to disk; held in server memory for 15 minutes)

We do not collect: payment card data, location data, vehicle telematics, browsing history, or any data from third-party social accounts.

4. How we use your data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your session
  • Run the CACCS scheduling algorithm with your vehicle profile and preferences
  • Display your charging history, costs, and savings in the dashboard
  • Compute annual value estimates for ERE certificates and solar self-consumption
  • Protect the service against credential-stuffing and brute-force attacks

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making with legal effects.

5. Cookies and local storage

SmartEV does not set HTTP cookies. We use the browser's localStorage API to store:

  • token — your JWT access token (expires after 7 days)
  • user — a JSON snapshot of your profile (name, email, supplier) for fast UI rendering
  • theme — your light/dark mode preference
  • cookie_consent — records whether you have dismissed the data notice banner

All localStorage items are stored only on your device. You can clear them at any time by logging out or clearing your browser's site data. We do not use analytics cookies, advertising pixels, or any third-party tracking scripts.

6. Data sharing and third parties

We share personal data with the following parties only:

  • Nederlandse Emissieautoriteit (NEa) — for ERE certificate issuance, your charging kWh totals are reported. No identifying personal information is included in the NEa submission.
  • No other third parties receive your personal data.

We use the following external services that may receive non-personal technical data:

  • Google Fonts — font files served from fonts.googleapis.com; your IP address is transmitted to Google when loading the Inter font family. You can opt out by enabling dark mode without reloading fonts (the font request is the same either way).
  • Ember Climate API — we fetch day-ahead wholesale electricity prices. No personal data is sent.

8. Your rights under GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU 2016/679), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — correct inaccurate data via Settings in the dashboard
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — delete your account and all associated data from the dashboard Settings page or by emailing privacy@smartev.nl
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — export your data as JSON via the dashboard Settings → Export my data
  • Right to restrict processing (Art. 18) — contact us at privacy@smartev.nl
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Right not to be subject to automated individual decision-making (Art. 22) — we do not make automated decisions with legal effects

You may exercise any of these rights by emailing privacy@smartev.nl or using the in-app controls in Settings. We will respond within one month.

9. Data security

We implement the following technical measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (work factor 12) — we cannot recover your plain-text password
  • Authentication uses HS256 JWT tokens with a 7-day expiry and cryptographically random signing key
  • Login endpoints are rate-limited to 10 failed attempts per IP per 15-minute window
  • All API endpoints (except public demo endpoints) require a valid Bearer token
  • The database is stored as a local SQLite file, readable only by the application process

The beta service runs over HTTP on localhost. Production deployment will require HTTPS. If you become aware of a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to security@smartev.nl.

10. International transfers

Your personal data is stored on the server where SmartEV is deployed. In the beta phase, this is a local development server within the European Union. The Ember Climate API servers and Google Fonts infrastructure may process non-personal technical data outside the EU, but no personal data is transferred to those services.

11. Children's privacy

SmartEV is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, please contact privacy@smartev.nl and we will delete the account promptly.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service evolves. If we make material changes, we will notify registered users by displaying a notice in the dashboard. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
