2. Description of the service

SmartEV provides a web-based platform for Dutch residential electric vehicle (EV) owners that:

  • Computes optimal overnight EV charging schedules using the CACCS algorithm, based on day-ahead EPEX SPOT electricity prices for Eneco and Essent dynamic tariff products
  • Displays real-time and forecast electricity price data for the Netherlands
  • Estimates annual financial savings from smart charging, ERE certificate earnings, and solar self-consumption matching
  • Stores vehicle profiles and charging session history for registered users

The Service does not currently communicate with physical EV chargers or take any automated action on connected hardware.
