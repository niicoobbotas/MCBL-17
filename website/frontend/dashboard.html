What are ERE certificates?

Hernieuwbaar Energiebewijs voor Elektrisch Rijden (HBE-OV) are Dutch renewable energy certificates earned by smart EV charging. Under the NEa 2026 framework, each kWh charged during validated green-energy periods earns a certificate sold to fuel suppliers who must prove renewable usage under the EU RED directive.

Requirements to earn ERE certificates:

MID-certified smart charger (e.g. Alfen Eve Single MID, EVBOX Elvi MID)
Dynamic electricity contract from a participating supplier (Eneco, Essent)
NEa registration via your energy supplier (process opens Q1 2026)
CACCS Grid Hero or Smart Balance charging profile for optimal certificate yield
Estimated revenue: €10–18 per month for a typical 15,000 km/year EV driver

Why solar integration matters after 2027

The Dutch net metering scheme (saldering) is being phased out:

64%2025
36%2026
9%2027+

After 2027, surplus solar exported to the grid earns very little. CACCS shifts your EV charging into the solar peak window (typically 10:00–15:00), so you consume your own electricity at full retail value instead of buying from the grid.

Typical self-consumption increase: 15–30% for a 4 kWp system
Best combined with Smart Balance or Grid Hero profile
Estimated net benefit: €15–30/month after 2027 for a 4 kWp system + EV
