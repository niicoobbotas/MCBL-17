How it works

Three steps to smarter charging

SmartEV connects your vehicle profile to today's live electricity market — no extra hardware needed to get started.

01

Connect your vehicle

Choose your EV from 20 pre-loaded Dutch top-sellers. We know your battery capacity, charge rate and real-world efficiency. Browse catalogue →

02

CACCS reads live prices

The algorithm fetches today's hourly Eneco / Essent dynamic tariffs and builds the cheapest charging schedule within your plug-in window.

03

Charge cheap, wake up full

Your car charges during the overnight valley — typically 01:00–05:00 — and is ready at your deadline. Nothing for you to do.
