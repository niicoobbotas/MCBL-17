App Features

Everything you need, in one place

The SmartEV app gives you full control of the CACCS algorithm with real-time feedback from today's Dutch electricity market.

Live Electricity Market

Hourly Eneco and Essent tariffs, the cheapest 3-hour window today, and your position vs. the daily average — all updated in real time.

Smart Charging Schedule

Set your target SoC and departure time. CACCS computes the per-hour plan and shows tonight's estimated saving before you plug in.

My Energy Bill

Monthly breakdown of cost vs. savings vs. a fixed contract. Running YTD totals and full session history. Export to CSV for your records.

ERE Certificate Tracker

Live tally of your ERE/kWh earned, pending registration with the NEa registry, and the € equivalent credited to your account.

Solar Self-Consumption

Enter your panel kWp. The app shifts charging to your solar peak, shows matched vs. exported kWh, and calculates the post-saldering value.

Push Notifications

Alert when a negative-price hour is coming. Reminder to plug in before the cheapest window. Confirmation when tonight's schedule is set.
