Assumptions & sources

Effective per-session range = WLTP × 0.85 × 0.70 (SoC band). Charger efficiency η = 0.92. CACCS uses Eindhoven zonal-load normalised proxy with β = 0.6.

ERE (NEa 2026): 0.333 ERE per kWh on grid electricity (50.5 % renewable share × 183 g/MJ × 3.6 MJ/kWh / 1000), 0.659 ERE per kWh on verified own renewables. Market price €0.30 / ERE (Groenbalans, Mar 2026). Net of 18 % inboeker commission.

Solar self-consumption (post-2027): 4 kWp PV typical (≈3 600 kWh/yr). Passive self-consumption rate 30 %, smart matching 70 %. Value per matched kWh = retail price − €0.07 feed-in tariff. Salderingsregeling ends 1 Jan 2027.
